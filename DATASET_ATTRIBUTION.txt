MAUS DATASET ATTRIBUTION

MAUS: A Dataset for Mental Workload Assessment on N-back Task Using Wearable Sensor.
IEEE DataPort DOI: 10.21227/q4td-yd35

This repository contains derived feature tables, evaluation outputs, and research summaries generated from MAUS. It does not redistribute the original MAUS.zip archive or the curated raw-recording subset.

To enable raw ECG, fingertip PPG, wrist PPG, and GSR/EDA waveform inspection, obtain the original dataset through its official IEEE DataPort record and follow the dataset-specific access and reuse terms. Place an authorized local copy at data/MAUS.zip. Do not commit or redistribute the dataset archives unless the provider's terms explicitly permit it.

Please cite the original MAUS dataset and observe its access and reuse terms in any poster, report, demonstration, or publication.
